# Brag Plan: Master Novelcraft

## Project Overview
**Master Novelcraft** is a portable, source-traceable AI writing skill for developing, drafting, revising, and learning to write novels and light novels. Built primarily from Alan Moore's writing advice with complementary prose, story-development, and screenwriting craft methods. The guiding principle: **the author leads; craft frameworks serve the story.**

## Key Features (15-Second Hook)
- **Author-led collaboration**: AI tutor, developmental editor, line editor, continuity steward — all preserve the author's voice and canon
- **Moore-inspired + international methods**: Alan Moore transcripts + Japanese character-first approaches + sitcom comedy frameworks
- **Three open author preferences**: When to fix theme, how strongly to outline, whether protagonist goals are obligatory — never asserted as defaults
- **Provenance-aware**: Distinguishes Moore-attributed transcripts, secondary commentary, contributed skills, novel adaptations, and original synthesis
- **No special software needed**: Works with ordinary Markdown; templates suffice for all workflows
- **Six development modes**: Architecture (ideas/outlines), Draft (new fiction), Comedy (character-rooted), Critique (ranked findings), Revise (full revision), Canon (world/character facts)
- **Light novel mode**: Optional contract selecting voice intimacy, visual clarity, alternating banter/interiority/action, expressive reactions, cultural expectations
- **Six reference categories**: Architecture & genre, Moore principles, character & conflict, scene & chapter, prose & dialogue, Japanese methods
- **Status tracking**: v2.1 release candidate with consistent active instructions

## What Makes It Unique
- **Source-traceable**: Every technique names its origin traceable to `references/source-ledger.md`
- **Competing lenses, not commandments**: Structural beat sheets, Japanese methods, character arcs, sitcom devices — all alternatives, used only what serves
- **Author preferences presented as choices**: Never mandatory global defaults; per-project selectable
- **Six non-operational source folders**: 19 `sw-*` folders, previous master skill, contributed documents preserved as reference material

## The Ask
A portable skill package that transforms AI into an author-led novel collaborator — no Story CLI, no dedicated paid API, no separate runtime needed. Just Markdown, craft references, and an AI assistant that can follow instructions.
