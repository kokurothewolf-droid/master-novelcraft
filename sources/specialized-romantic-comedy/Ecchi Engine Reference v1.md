# Ecchi Engine Reference v1: Comparative Craft Extract

**Purpose:** compare three romantic-comedy architectures and their transferable prose techniques. Public extract; private project applications have been removed.

**Sources:** TV Tropes series pages for High School DxD and Mato Seihei no Slave; the High School DxD wiki on Dress Break / Pailingual / Nyuutron Beam Cannon; episode summaries and reviews of Monster Musume (ANN, Anime UK News, oprainfall, TV Tropes gag catalog); mechanic breakdowns of the Slave / Reward system; TV Tropes trope definitions for Fanservice, Accidental Pervert, Nosebleed, Clothing Damage, Power Perversion Potential, Marshmallow Hell.

## 1. The three architectures

These are not the same joke told three ways. They solve the same problem (maximum provocation, zero consummation) with three incompatible machines, and each one produces a different kind of scene.

### 1a. Monster Musume: the husbandry engine

**The machine:** an administrative program. A government exchange act installs monster girls in a human household as a legal homestay, and the same act forbids interspecies sex and interspecies harm. The prohibition is the load-bearing wall. Provocation runs at maximum because consummation is illegal, and nobody in the house can even pretend the alternative exists.

**Where the eroticism comes from:** species physiology, treated as hard fact. Not "she has ears" but "her body does this, therefore the evening goes wrong." Confirmed devices:

- Shedding and molting. The lamia sheds and cannot peel her own back; he does it; the scene is intimate and ends with him in a neck brace.
- Egg-laying as an episode event, with the household panicking, an outsider trying to film it, and the girls closing ranks to protect her.
- Full-moon heat. Every liminal's libido spikes at once; he spends the night avoiding three of them and institutes a curfew rule the next morning.
- Physiology as obstacle: a spider's lungs are in her abdomen so she cannot be submerged, and the others drag her into the bath anyway; a centaur runs at 38°C and burns him while warming him; a slime dissolves in rain.
- Species-appropriate erotics: grooming, warmth-seeking, tails with their own opinions, purring, weight and strength differences.

**Comedy mode:** absurdist escalation and endurance. The nudity is deliberately played as *ridiculous rather than arousing* (a reviewer's words: "too ridiculous to actually be arousing"). Nobody ever reaches for a towel. One character "is actually more embarrassed about wearing clothes than her being completely naked."

**The male lead:** not a pervert. A competent, durable, unflappable caretaker with a high pain threshold. His arousal is on the page but he never initiates, because to initiate is to be the villain. He is loved because he treats each of them as an ordinary woman with a normal life, which is stated as the reason in nearly every write-up.

**Chapter shape:** biology event → house-wide chaos → an outsider intrudes → the household closes ranks (and someone gets decked) → one sincere beat where the girls coach a housemate through something embarrassing → order restored by a rule, and the rule costs him something.

**Reward economy:** none. He is paid in injuries, chores, and affection.

**Competition device:** a marriage amendment. One of them will be selected, which converts affection into a contest with an end date.

### 1b. High School DxD: the lust-is-power engine

**The machine:** a pact. He is killed, resurrected as a devil, and given a power source that runs on desire. His desire is not a complication of the plot; it is the fuel line.

**Where the eroticism comes from:** mechanics that are explicitly, unashamedly sexual and mechanically load-bearing. Confirmed devices:

- Dress Break: contact, a magic circle, a finger snap, and the target's clothing (armour, restraints, even curse effects) falls off. Developed specifically to fight an all-female peerage. Works because demonic power is shaped by imagination, so it is literally his libido weaponised, and it therefore does not work on men.
- Pailingual: an area spell that makes women's breasts speak and reveal their plans, bypassing mind-reading defences because it does not target the mind. Banned from competitive play after complaints.
- Nyuutron Beam Cannon: tail attached to a woman's breast, bust size displayed on his gauntlet as a damage readout.
- Power transfer and replenishment performed through erotic contact, with a visible cost (the breasts shrink afterwards).
- Balance Breaker unlocked by groping.

**Comedy mode:** serial escalation and proud self-parody. "How much can he power up using Rias's breasts?" is treated as an open design question by the show itself. A breast god and an Oppai Dragon children's mascot both exist in-universe. The series parodies its own tropes and lampshades them.

**The male lead:** an *openly, proudly* lecherous protagonist. The working archetype name is the chivalrous pervert: he leers constantly, admits it, is not ashamed, and is nonetheless respectful, and the one thing that makes him genuinely lethal is anyone harming the women he cares about. He is not a bland everyman covering for the audience; he has an ambition and states it (Harem King), which makes the harem ending a foregone conclusion and moves all the drama into *how*.

**Structural note:** roughly half of it is a straight battle shonen. The lewd comedy is the release valve for a story that also goes dark, and the author has said the final arc returns to the fan-service roots. Tone swings are the format, not a failure of it.

**Bath/undress culture:** nudity around him is ordinary; baths are a standing set piece. Two useful archetypes are used in tandem: the shameless fanservice girl who knows exactly what she is doing, and the innocent one who genuinely does not.

### 1c. Chained Soldier: the contractual-ecchi engine

**The machine:** an obligation. A world rule makes power gender-locked (only women can use it), so a man can only be powerful by being *lent* power by a woman through a control ability. The lending comes with a supernatural debt: after the fight, the master is magically compelled to give him a reward scaled to how much power he used.

**Where the eroticism comes from:** price and ritual, not accident. Confirmed devices:

- The reward is owed, not stumbled into. "Based on his efforts and performance, in an equivalent manner, based on said slave's inherent desires." A bigger fight means a bigger reward, and the reward must be paid promptly or the master cannot move freely. What reads as a gag is also a tactical constraint: they cannot fight forever without paying the toll.
- Rewards are personalised per woman and function as each relationship's signature act, because the shape of the reward reflects the master's personality and his unconscious wants. One woman's reward is a bandaged wound; another's is far more explicit. The catalogue of signatures is the harem's characterisation.
- The man can be lent to other women, and each lending produces a different form and power, which is how new heroines enter and why they each get a turn.
- Collars, chains, and being used as a mount are outright text rather than subtext.

**Comedy mode:** humiliation-flavoured and femdom by construction. The women are the ones obligated to perform, so the comedy of embarrassment runs the other direction from Monster Musume: their reluctance, their mortification, and eventually their enthusiasm.

**The male lead:** a house husband. He cooks, cleans, and is genuinely treasured for domestic competence; several reviews single out that his domestic skill is what the girls value. His power is borrowed, and he is a vessel rather than a source.

**Structural note:** the gendered power rule produces a matriarchal military institution, which gives the erotics a social frame rather than a domestic one.

## 2. The shared toolbox

### Translatable to prose

| Device | What it is |
|---|---|
| Nosebleed | Visual shorthand for arousal, from mild trickle to gushing. Based on the old wives' tale that arousal raises blood pressure. |
| Accidental Pervert | The universe conspires: slips, rugs, wind, wrong doors. "His life is a Fanservice-laden version of Cold Turkeys Are Everywhere." |
| Marshmallow Hell | A shorter character smothered in a taller woman's chest, unable to breathe and unable to see. |
| Clothing Damage | Clothes as ongoing casualties; transformations shred them; the Nudifier weapon. |
| Power Perversion Potential | Powers used for erotic ends; "Fantastic Arousal" from unusual body parts. |
| Shameless and innocent archetype pairing | One heroine who knows exactly what she is doing, one who does not. |
| The prohibition | Law, blood loss, exhaustion, or interruption keeps it non-explicit. |

### Not translatable (anime-only grammar)

- Censorship by object placement, light rays, and framing. In prose there is no frame to obstruct, so the equivalent is *narrative attention*: what he notices and what he refuses to name.
- Chibi expressions, cross-popping veins, colour failure, sweatdrops.
- Imagine-spots, narrator annotation boxes, and "pop-up" gags about the characters.
- Panel-based comics timing. Prose needs a different beat structure for the same joke: setup, escalation, interruption, consequence.
