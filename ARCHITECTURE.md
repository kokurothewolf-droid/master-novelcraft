# Architecture, priorities and unresolved choices

## Four layers

1. **Author layer:** explicit task, story concept, fiction form, canon, POV/tense, desired reader effect and voice. This is the authority for creative decisions.
2. **Selection layer:** pick development method, genre, light novel or general novel mode, and active craft modules only when relevant.
3. **Craft layer:** story design, characters, scenes, prose, comedy, worldbuilding, Japanese and international transferable techniques.
4. **Continuity layer:** project bible, knowledge-at-time, timeline, changing relationships, world rules, foreshadowing and unresolved canon issues.

## Incompatible source doctrines

There is no neutral theorem proving theme must be fixed early, all stories must be plotted before drafting, or all protagonists must transform. Therefore the master **does not encode any of those as universal laws**. It records the competing approaches under `references/author-decisions.md` and allows explicit per-project selections.

## Screenwriting to novel translation filter

Take a technique only if it improves prose-fiction reader experience. Replace camera instructions with perception, interior monologue and embodied detail; production timing with prose pacing; scene cuts with chapter/paragraph transition choices; act-outs with organic narrative promises; audience-role comedy with the reader's inferential participation; season bibles with series and volume planning. Do not convert page-per-minute formulas or screenplay dialog into novel prose templates.

## Japanese breadth requirement

`references/japanese-coverage.md` audits the explicit Japanese-related occurrences across every non-opera SW source file, not just the `sw-japanese-screenwriting` folder. It distinguishes source types, including Japanese screenplay formatting that **does not** belong in novel prose and Japan-related historical examples that do not teach Japanese storycraft. Particular attention goes to the Japanese methods source and the Sakamoto Yuji relationship-writing case study.

## Source hierarchy

User's own manuscript/canon > explicit current instructions > chosen craft path > transferable techniques > raw examples. Moore's directly attributed teachings are not falsely blended with LitReactor commentary or with later independent craft frameworks.

## Dependency policy

All active guidance runs in normal Markdown. Original skill references to scripts, Story CLI, missing sibling skills, a watch daemon, proprietary production workflows, and industry formatting are source context only, not runtime prerequisites.

## Romantic-comedy specialization (v2.1)

This module is opt-in. Comparative sources and prose adaptations are separate from author-approved canon. The public source extract omits private character applications and project proposals. An incomplete private source is documented as incomplete rather than represented as a full archived original. See `references/romantic-comedy-source-ledger.md` and `PUBLIC_EDITION_NOTES.md`.
