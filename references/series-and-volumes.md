# Chapters, volumes, series engines and long-form continuity

**Sources:** `sw-series-engine-bible`, `sw-series-structure`, `sw-series-case-studies`, selected `sw-chinese-series-practice`, `plot-structure(1).md`, `story-zoom(1).md` and `character-management(1).md`. These were designed largely for television; all below are **novel adaptations**.

## The story engine

A series engine is a **repeatable source of interesting story situations**: an inn's house rules, a guild economy, rival families, a school, an adventuring party, a research institution, a neighborhood, a cursed household, or an unresolved emotional triangle. For each engine ask:

- Why must these characters keep interacting?
- Which personal aims clash in renewable ways?
- What shared resources, duties, geography or history make avoidance impossible?
- What can change permanently without destroying the premise?
- What recurring pleasures does the reader reasonably expect?

A **repeatable situation** is different from resetting consequences. A character's standing with another person must not revert merely because a new volume begins.

## Layer the unit scales

**Scene:** localized experience, shift, discovery, expression or purposeful stillness. **Chapter:** a readable emotional/narrative unit; may end in tension, resonance, humor or quiet completion. **Volume:** a substantial promise paid off, with longer questions sustained. **Series:** cumulative alteration of relationships, places, themes and history. Use explicit scene/volume maps when author wants structure; exploratory authors can discover and then label this hierarchy in revision.

## Braid without random shuffling

For each major thread record owner, immediate state, next pressure, reader promise, active chapters, reveal timing and the causal or thematic reason for switching. The source describes multiple competing ways to weave A/B/C strands, including character-first approaches that reject fixed A/B/C labels. Choose one. Changing viewpoint should yield new knowledge, emotional contrast, needed relief or an intentional surprise. Avoid cutting away merely because a TV formula would require it.

## Information management

Track facts under four headings: **reader knows**, **viewpoint character knows**, **other character knows**, and **author knows but has not revealed**. Build dramatic irony with asymmetry. A reveal should prompt a new choice, reinterpret a prior encounter or change expectations. A mystery may remain unresolved when the author deliberately promises ambiguity rather than a solution.

## Character-based endings and cliffhangers

Possible chapter/volume endings: an altered relationship, new urgent question, revealed information, person leaving, found object, changed interpretation of a familiar line, unresolved choice, affectionate afterglow or comic callback. Avoid fake threats, constant cliffhangers or mandatory end-of-chapter shocks. The reader's interest can persist through a gentler invitation.

## Relationship accumulations inspired by Japanese source case studies

Sakamoto Yuji example: everyday objects, reused locations, recurring phrases and shifting date specificity reveal emotional progress without a melodramatic villain. For prose, record a motif table: object or phrase, first meaning, next context, reversed meaning, anticipated payoff. The case study's reported numbers describe one film; there is no suggested ratio of short to long scenes for a novel.

## Portable volume ledger

Use `templates/volume-plan.md` and `templates/canon-change.md`. Record: current volume objective (if any), relationship developments, world-state alterations, resolved and open promises, revealed secrets, timeline anchors, future foreshadowing, and characters' current knowledge. Preserve potential arcs separately from established events.

## Guardrails

No television pilot page models, teaser/act labels, advertisement breaks, length in minutes, cast budgets or television season quotas. Do not invent missing `series-continuity` modules or commands. A novel may be episodic, nonlinear, quiet, domestic or highly serialized; choose according to its contract.
